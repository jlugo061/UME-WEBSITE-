Signature build

The blacked-out helm

We replace the screen area of your console with one black Starboard panel, cut to fit your dash exactly. The displays sit flush in a single clean black face, and the rest of the console stays factory.

  1. 01Measure. We template the console's screen area and lay out every display opening.
  2. 02Cut. The black Starboard panel is cut to the exact shape of your dash.
  3. 03Sand & polish. Edges and face are finished by hand.
  4. 04Install. Bonded with marine adhesive and bolted in, then the displays go in flush.

How a build goes

From bare console to sea trial

  1. 1

    Walk the boat

    Call or text us a photo. We look at the hull, the console and how you fish or cruise, then spec the electronics around it.

  2. 2

    Lay out the helm

    Display sizes, flush or pod mount, switch panel and where every piece sits. You approve the layout before anything gets cut.

  3. 3

    Install & wire

    In our indoor Davie shop or at your marina. Labeled, loom-wrapped runs on a clean NMEA 2000 backbone, tested on a scope.

  4. 4

    Sea trial & handoff

    We calibrate autopilot, compass and radar underway, then walk you through every screen before you run it on your own.
